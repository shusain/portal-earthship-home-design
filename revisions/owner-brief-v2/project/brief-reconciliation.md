## Critical reconciliations

- **Roof area:** 800 sf garden does not fill 1,250 sf roof half. Keep garden plus 450 sf of the service zone beneath PC before overhangs. Bathroom is enclosed under the opaque part; glazed garden screen is a coordination proposal.
- **Underground / escape:** model assumes retained walls and an exposed roof with a south daylight/escape court. Burial depth, floor datum, court-to-grade route, slope and drains require the APN/topographic/soil reports. No parcel fit has been established.
- **Backfill sequence:** PDF phase 5 precedes roof phase 6. Do not infer that an unrestrained retaining wall may be fully backfilled. Structural engineer must release temporary bracing or permanent diaphragm restraint and controlled lifts; HP6 includes that additional release.
- **Opt-out:** current county program still requires a permit and other agency approvals. Option 2 does not offer a Certificate of Occupancy, and has an administrative final site visit. Private engineering/inspection remains mandatory under the owner brief, not a claim that County waived all safety obligations.
- **Panel procurement:** 100 x 25 ft is a comparison, not the cheapest proven design. PC runs slope across roughly 27 ft including overhangs; supplier/engineer must confirm a compatible panel-length/joint system or revise dimensions to stock. Do not butt/lap multiwall sheets from a sketch.
- **Roof pitch:** provisional 10% = 1.2 in/ft, replacing the old 1/4 in/ft roof. PC supplier/model may require a different approved pitch.
- **Owner role / schedule:** delegated PM/trade delivery, no owner DIY assumption. Use PDF weeks 1–72 overall plus closeout; it is not the earlier construction-only 21–37 week schedule. Dates remain relative until a start is agreed.
- **Acquired assets:** land and off-grid power remain acquired; house electrical/interface work retained. Water/septic approvals are assumptions until written copies are on file. No actual project expenses or vendor promises invented.
