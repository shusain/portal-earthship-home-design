Schedule basis

Use the owner's weeks 1–72 overall envelope, plus close-out after week 72. Site/construction phases are roughly project weeks 22–72; design, approvals and long-lead procurement precede them. This replaces the old 21–37 week hired-crew estimate.

Active crew weeks below are effort/(crew × 40 hours), not a delivery promise. Different trades overlap, hold-point releases can stop work, and final supplier dates/engineered sequence may lengthen the envelope. In particular, full backfill needs engineered restraint; it cannot be released merely because waterproofing was inspected.
