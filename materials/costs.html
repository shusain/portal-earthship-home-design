US purchasing basis

Nominal 2×4 = actual 1½″ × 3½″; nominal 2×6 = actual 1½″ × 5½″. Stud spacing 16″ on center. Header study uses two 2×6 plies with a ½″ plywood spacer. Lumber board counts are volume-derived budgeting equivalents; grade, spans, member cuts and plate splices need final design.

Sheets and boards: 4′ × 8′. Concrete and aggregate: cubic yards. Membranes: mils and full rolls. Pipe: nominal inches and 10′ sticks. Quantities are rounded for buying, with explicit waste. Engineering source geometry retains its reviewed integer-mm datums; no metric stock sizes are specified.

What still needs a quote

Ready-mix transport to Portal, rebar/formwork, engineered joists and transfer supports, waterproofing assembly, deep-burial pipe class, excavation, utilities and installation. The thin-wall drain link is a price comparison only and is not approved for the proposed burial depth. A 36″ door source aperture is not a finalized prehung rough opening.

Four 10′ × 100′ membrane rolls are reserved for four approximately 66′ roof courses. Three rolls based only on total square footage would not establish a viable seam layout. New roof-approved insulation is priced as two 3″ layers; reclaimed boards are not used.
