## Labor and schedule assumptions

- Person-hours are the sum of individual workers’ time. A 4-person crew for 40 hours each equals 160 person-hours; crew size is used to derive duration, never multiplied into the labor bill again.
- Rates are per worker-hour and include an authored allowance for trade employer burden, overhead and normal margin. Travel, minimum callouts and remote-site accommodation may require quote adjustments. Equipment costs are separate; operator labor is in the phase rows.
- Durations show active crew-work weeks plus separate cure/drying/inspection waits. Schedule depends on preceding phases; parallel crews can overlap. Do not add every phase duration as if all trades were sequential.
- The hired-crew model assumes each phase crew is available for 40 productive hours per worker per week when scheduled. Its 2–4 week buffer is for ordinary coordination/weather interruptions, not preconstruction design, permit issuance or exceptional supplier delays.
- Concrete release, temporary bracing and backfill sequence are controlled by the structural/envelope designers. Waiting windows here do not authorize loading concrete after a fixed number of days.
- Owner-assisted savings assume all marked phase labor is replaced by owner effort. The scenario allows 1.35 times professional person-hours, at 40 combined owner-team hours/week; the resulting extra weeks are calculated along the dependency path. Fabrication/specialist work may still require paid help.
- Owner-managed cases exclude a GC coordination fee but still require procurement, supervision and inspection coordination. Owner project-management hours are additional to the stated owner hands-on hours.
- Materials retain supplier-linked benchmarks, quantity/waste rounding and clearly identified provisional allowances. This is a construction estimate; actual material invoices and local labor bids are not yet available.

## Phase scope / dependencies

- **Layout, excavation and subgrade** (after: mobilization): Operator and ground worker labor; machine hire/fuel/transport priced separately. Assumes no blasting/dewatering.
- **Footing forms, reinforcement and placement** (after: excavate): Concrete material in purchasing table; pump/forms hire separate. Inspection and release allowance included in waiting time.
- **Retaining-wall forms, steel, pour and strip** (after: footings): Site-engineered retaining shell. Waiting is a schedule allowance, not approval to strip/load/backfill at a particular concrete age; engineer sets release.
- **Underslab prep, service coordination and slab** (after: walls): Includes underfloor pipe/sleeve installation labor; plumbing/electrical rough rows exclude this work. Cure/inspection waiting allowance.
- **Wall waterproofing and footing drainage** (after: walls): Membrane, protection, drain and outlet work; material package counted separately. Inspection before concealment.
- **Roof framing, transfer supports and deck** (after: slab): Engineered joist/transfer design required. Lift/crane hire separate; trade labor included here.
- **Roof insulation, membrane, flashing and gutters** (after: roof_structure): Dry-in and envelope inspection allowance; materials priced once in purchasing table.
- **Interior partition framing and door blocking** (after: roof_weather): Owner-assisted option substitutes owner effort only here and in finish phases; structural roof/retaining work remains hired.
- **Exterior windows and entry doors** (after: roof_weather): Weather flashing and escape-opening verification; no window material double count.
- **Above-slab plumbing rough-in** (after: partitions, openings): House supply/waste lines and tests only; excludes septic/well. Underslab labor is in slab phase.
- **House electrical rough-in** (after: partitions, openings): House panel, branch circuits and existing-power interface checks. No PV/battery/generator purchase or utility extension.
- **Heat pump and ventilation rough-in** (after: partitions, openings): Ducts, equipment installation and rough connections; equipment supply counted in materials.
- **Rough trade inspection / correction window** (after: plumbing_rough, electrical_rough, hvac_rough): Waiting window only, not a fee or another bill for trade hours; normal corrections covered in phase ranges.
- **Drywall, plaster and painting** (after: rough_inspection): Interior finish labor only. Drying time is separate from person-hours.
- **Cabinets, counters, flooring and interior doors** (after: linings): Fit-out labor; cabinetry, flooring and door materials already priced. Specialized countertop fabrication may remain subcontracted in owner option.
- **Plumbing trim and fixtures** (after: finishes): Fixtures/trim labor, separate from rough-in hours; supply in materials.
- **Electrical trim, alarms and lighting** (after: finishes): Trim and testing; no new off-grid generation/storage equipment.
- **HVAC final connections and balancing** (after: linings): Mechanical commissioning labor; equipment cost remains in materials.
- **Engineered backfill and immediate final grading** (after: waterproof, roof_structure): Roof diaphragm/retaining system and waterproofing must be released before backfill. Engineer's sequence governs; machines priced separately.
- **Punch list, cleanup and final inspections** (after: plumbing_trim, electrical_trim, hvac_trim, backfill): Multitrade closeout and cleanup, excluding duplicate HVAC balancing/trim labor. Inspection scheduling risk is also in overall buffer.
