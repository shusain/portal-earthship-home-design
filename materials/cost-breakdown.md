## Imperial stock and geometry

All buying units are US customary: boards in feet and inches, 4 ft x 8 ft sheets/boards, rolls, linear feet (lf), square feet (sf), cubic feet (cf), cubic yards (cy), pounds (lb), and each. Blender displays Imperial units. Exact dressed 2x4 = 1.5 x 3.5 in; dressed 2x6 = 1.5 x 5.5 in. Stud spacing = 16 in on center. Header assembly = two 2x6 plies + 1/2 in plywood spacer. Header grade/size and roof framing remain engineering reservations. Plans retain integer-mm computation solely to preserve reviewed source topology; that is not the stock specification.

The original rounded framing was rebuilt and remeasured with exact inch-based sections. Header lumber and spacer plywood are no longer hidden in the 2x4 volume equivalent. A 10 ft 2x4 budget count includes cutting/waste, but a cut/splice schedule is still required; long plates may need 12/16 ft stock. Buy 10 ft studs for the current 9 ft datum and cut to the detailed plate/ceiling design; do not assume a standard precut length fits.

Roof deck uses a 23/32 in rated sheet allowance; ceiling purchasing assumes 5/8 in sag-resistant board. Source roof/ceiling/membrane layer thicknesses remain schematic and must be coordinated to the selected tested assembly. The thermal design is not code certified.
